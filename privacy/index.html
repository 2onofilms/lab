Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy covers 2ONO Lab (2onofilms.github.io/lab) and the automated Instagram messages sent by the account @2onofilms (owner: Valerio Del Popolo, Rome, Italy).

What the Instagram automation does

When you comment a keyword under a post by @2onofilms, an automated system replies to your comment and sends you a direct message with a link to a free resource. Before sending the link it checks, through Instagram's official API, whether you follow @2onofilms.

What data is processed

  • Your Instagram-scoped user ID and username, the ID and text of your comment, and the messages or buttons you send to @2onofilms.
  • Whether you follow @2onofilms, as returned by the Instagram API at the moment you tap the button.

This data is used only to send you the requested resource and to count, in aggregate, how many people requested it. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for marketing, and not used for advertising.

Where and for how long

Data is processed through Meta's Instagram API and stored on Cloudflare (Workers KV). Event records are deleted automatically after 30 days. The 2ONO Lab website itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Fonts may be loaded from Fontshare.

Your rights and deletion

You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time by sending a direct message with the word DELETE to @2onofilms on Instagram. Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, correct and object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
